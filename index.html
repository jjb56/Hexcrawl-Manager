Hotkeys

ctrl+n to start a new world.

ctrl+o to open a saved world.

ctrl+s to save your world.

ctrl+z to undo.

ctrl+y to redo.

ctrl+= or scroll up on the map to zoom in.

ctrl+- or scroll down on the map to zoom out.

ctrl+0 to reset the map view.


win+. (windows) or ctrl+cmd+space (mac) to open emoji window in text boxes.
